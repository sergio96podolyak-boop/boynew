# Gameplay and Retention Roadmap

## Vision

PoMarket should evolve from a charming single-screen business sim into a richer neighborhood market experience without losing its approachable core. The gameplay roadmap should focus on making the store feel more alive, more varied, and more rewarding to revisit.

## Gameplay Pillars

### 1. Stronger daily loop

The game should reward regular play with meaningful but lightweight progress. Daily pushes should feel satisfying, not mandatory.

### 2. More store personality

As the store grows, the player should see more evidence of their business identity through layout, products, and atmosphere.

### 3. More meaningful progression

Each upgrade should create clearer value: more capacity, better efficiency, more revenue, or more atmosphere.

### 4. Better customer rhythm

Customers should not feel like a static stream. They should create a more dynamic rhythm of shopping, waiting, checkout, and reward.

## Near-Term Gameplay Additions

### Phase 1: Core polish

- Improve upgrade clarity and visible payoff.
- Add stronger state feedback for shelves, stock, and checkout.
- Introduce more varied customer behavior patterns.
- Add milestone-based reward bursts.

### Phase 2: Store depth

- Add new store zones and department types.
- Expand the product and shelf system.
- Create more meaningful store-level unlocks.
- Introduce limited-time seasonal or themed offers.

### Phase 3: Long-term engagement

- Add recurring events and special event weeks.
- Expand progression into more branching business goals.
- Introduce collection or catalog systems for store upgrades and cosmetics.
- Add stronger retention loops around daily challenges and streaks.

## Retention Strategy

### Daily loop

- Daily bonus.
- Small recurring goals.
- Short path to meaningful progress.
- Reward for returning even on low-intensity days.

### Weekly loop

- Event weeks.
- Rotating challenge targets.
- Limited-time store themes.
- Temporary unlockable cosmetics or bonuses.

### Long-term motivation

- Visible store evolution.
- Achievements and mastery goals.
- Prestige or legacy systems that make future growth feel special.

## Progression Design Goals

Every progression system should answer three questions:

1. What changed?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What is the next thing to chase?

If the player cannot answer those quickly, the system is too opaque.

## Recommended Content Hooks

- Weekend rush events.
- Holiday themes.
- Special customer requests.
- New store departments.
- Better reward cadence for consecutive play.

## Retention Principles

- Keep short-session value high.
- Make progress legible.
- Reward continuity without punishing absence.
- Avoid overloading the player with too many systems at once.

## Suggested KPI Targets

While the product is still early, the most useful metrics are:

- session length,
- return rate,
- upgrade conversion,
- reward claim rate,
- progression completion rate,
- retention after day 7 and day 30.

## Recommendation

The best roadmap is not to add complexity for its own sake. The best path is to deepen the business fantasy through a few high-quality systems: richer store growth, more varied customer moments, clearer rewards, and a stronger daily rhythm.

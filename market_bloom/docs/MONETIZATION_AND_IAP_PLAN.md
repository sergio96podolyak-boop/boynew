# Monetization and IAP Plan

## Monetization Principles

PoMarket should use monetization to support quality and retention, not to undermine the core loop. The current product already has a solid foundation for this: ad reward flows, store offers, and an abstraction layer that supports future expansion.

## Recommended Monetization Mix

### 1. Rewarded ads

Rewarded ads should remain a core monetization tool for short-term value.

Recommended uses:

- instant coin reward,
- bonus cash for a limited time,
- extra progression support in a session.

This should feel optional and helpful, not mandatory.

### 2. Premium removal

A premium no-ads offer is appropriate for players who want a cleaner and more uninterrupted experience.

Suggested positioning:

- remove interruptions,
- preserve the experience,
- respect players who prefer a premium feel.

### 3. Cosmetic and convenience IAP

IAP should focus on convenience and personalization rather than raw power.

Good examples:

- starter bundles,
- coin packs,
- cosmetic store themes,
- convenience bundles that help with early progression.

The product should avoid making progression feel “pay-to-win.”

## IAP Offer Structure

### Core offers

- No-ads premium pass or premium unlock.
- Coin bundle for players who want to accelerate early momentum.
- Starter bundle for first-time players who want a stronger opening experience.

### Optional future offers

- seasonal cosmetic packs,
- themed decoration bundles,
- premium event access,
- limited-time convenience boosts.

## Economy Design Guidance

The economy should be carefully paced so that:

- early progression feels rewarding,
- mid-game upgrades feel meaningful,
- premium purchases are optional rather than necessary,
- ad rewards remain a helper, not a trap.

## Monetization Guardrails

- Do not gate core progression behind paid content.
- Preserve a strong free-to-play experience.
- Keep premium purchases aligned with player value and comfort.
- Make sure rewarded ads never feel exploitative.

## Recommended Pricing Strategy

Pricing should be simple and transparent:

- small pack for casual players,
- medium pack for regular players,
- premium offer for dedicated players.

The pricing structure should reflect the value of convenience and a smoother experience rather than a dramatic power boost.

## Live Ops Monetization Opportunities

The monetization layer should support live events, for example:

- limited-time reward bundles,
- special holiday offers,
- milestone celebration sales,
- event-based cosmetics.

This creates a strong cadence without making the game feel permanently sales-driven.

## Compliance and Store Readiness

Before launch or major expansion, the following should be addressed:

- privacy consent and disclosures,
- app store compliance,
- store product configuration,
- server-side receipt validation for IAP,
- analytics and event tracking for monetization performance.

## Recommendation

PoMarket should stay lightweight and player-friendly in its monetization. The best approach is a blend of rewarded ads, a premium no-ads option, and optional convenience or cosmetic IAP. That keeps the game accessible while allowing the business to grow sustainably.
